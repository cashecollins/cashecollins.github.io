02 — Ideals

Four ideals
I lead by.

The principles behind how I build software and lead teams, and the quotes that keep them front of mind.

Ideal 01

Row in the same direction

A unified group of 5 people executing a good plan together is more effective than a split group executing separate great plans.

Cashe Collins, “The Dota Principle”

Team alignment is key to success. Even when our individual approaches differ, everyone should work toward the same goals with clarity and cohesion. That means no silos: the team moves forward together.

  • Apes together, strong. Caesar, Planet of the Apes
  • Best idea wins.

Ideal 02

Stop when you're proud

You are the master of your destiny. You can influence, direct and control your own environment. You can make your life what you want it to be.

Napoleon Hill

Quality over finishing for its own sake. Take ownership of your work and make sure it meets a high standard before moving on. Rather than rushing to done, aim for results you're proud of, personally and professionally.

  • If I had asked people what they wanted, they would have said faster horses. Henry Ford
  • As a baseball umpire, every call you make will only be right 50% of the time. My dad, after a 40-year-old dad chewed out 15-year-old me for calling a strike at a 10-year-olds' game

Ideal 03

Slow is smooth, smooth is fast

Slow and steady wins the race.

Aesop, The Tortoise and the Hare

The eternal battle of quality vs. speed. Precision and thoughtfulness beat haste over the long run. Planning, executing carefully, and refining the process avoids costly mistakes and rework, because real efficiency comes from mastery, not shortcuts.

  • Measure twice, cut once.

Ideal 04

Business integrity

Actions speak louder than words. And sometimes inaction speaks louder than both of them.

Matthew Good

Honesty, ethics, and accountability in every decision. Operate transparently, uphold the company's values, and keep the trust of clients, partners, and colleagues. Integrity means doing the right thing, even when no one is watching.

  • Consistently doing what you say you will do is the foundation of integrity. Skip Prichard
  • Some of the worst things imaginable have been done with the best of intentions. Alan Grant, Jurassic Park
